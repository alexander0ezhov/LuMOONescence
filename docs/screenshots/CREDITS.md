## If you reuse a screenshot from here

- The pictures of this app (`layout-editor`, `stream-menu`, `desktop-control`, `layouts-screen`) may be
  used under the GPL v3, which this project is under: keep the notice, and the source goes with it.
- `game-supertuxkart.jpg` is **CC BY-SA 4.0**: credit SuperTuxKart, link the licence, and pass the
  image on under the same licence.
- The two illustrations (`game.jpg`, `hold-and-drag.jpg`) are ours to give away under the GPL v3.

## The same rule inside the app

The app shows the licenses of everything it is built from under **Settings → About → Open source
licenses**. Images are not shipped inside the APK except for the launcher icon and the layouts the
app creates, which are this project's own.
